# Third-party components

Lumine is built on the work below. Each part keeps its own licence; the full texts are in [`app/src/main/assets/licenses/`](app/src/main/assets/licenses/) and are also shown inside the app (the About page). Fonts marked "cut down" are subsets (only the glyphs the app uses), some renamed as the licence requires.
